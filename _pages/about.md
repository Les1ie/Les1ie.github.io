I am **Zhen Wu** (吴臻), a PhD student at [Southeast University](https://www.seu.edu.cn/), advised by [Jinghui Zhang](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-9067-7896). I received my M.S. degree from [Soochow University](https://www.suda.edu.cn/) and my B.S. degree from [Jiangxi University of Finance and Economics](https://www.jxufe.edu.cn/).

My research interests include **information diffusion prediction**, **popularity prediction**, **paper recommendation**, and **graph neural networks**, with recent work on few-shot action recognition and image restoration.
